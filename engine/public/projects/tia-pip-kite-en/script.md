# Tia & Pip: The Kite in the Tree

- Format: 9:16 kids story, ~35s, English
- Audience: kids 2-6 and parents
- Style: bright, soft-3D cartoon look, original characters (code-drawn SVG, no AI images)
- Characters:
  - **Tia** — cheerful girl, two puffy pigtails with red bobbles, yellow shirt, blue overalls
  - **Pip** — little sky-blue elephant with pink ears and an orange scarf
- Problem: Tia's kite gets stuck in a tall tree. Solution: teamwork — Tia climbs on Pip's back, Pip lifts his trunk.
- Hook (0-3s): This is Tia! She loves her bright red kite.
- CTA / moral: Friends help each other!

## Narration
1. This is Tia! She loves flying her bright red kite.
2. Whoosh! A big wind blows... and the kite gets stuck in the tall tree!
3. Tia jumps and jumps. But the kite is too high.
4. Then along comes Pip, the little elephant. "I can help!"
5. Tia climbs onto Pip's back. Pip lifts his long trunk up, up, up!
6. Got it! The kite is free! Hooray!
7. Now Tia and Pip fly the kite together. Friends help each other!
